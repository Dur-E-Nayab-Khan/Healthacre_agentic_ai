Hypertension Management Guideline (Sample Reference Document)
================================================================

1. Blood pressure categories
Normal: systolic <120 and diastolic <80 mmHg.
Elevated: systolic 120-129 and diastolic <80 mmHg.
Stage 1 hypertension: systolic 130-139 or diastolic 80-89 mmHg.
Stage 2 hypertension: systolic >=140 or diastolic >=90 mmHg.

2. Initial management
For Stage 1 hypertension with low estimated 10-year cardiovascular risk,
lifestyle modification alone (sodium restriction, DASH-style diet,
physical activity, weight loss, alcohol moderation) for 3-6 months is
reasonable before adding medication.

3. Pharmacologic therapy
First-line agents include thiazide diuretics, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and
calcium channel blockers. ACE inhibitors and ARBs are preferred in
patients with diabetes and albuminuria, or with heart failure with
reduced ejection fraction. Avoid combining an ACE inhibitor with an ARB.

4. Follow-up
Recheck blood pressure within 1 month of starting or adjusting therapy.
Once at goal, routine follow-up every 3-6 months is reasonable for stable
patients.

5. Resistant hypertension
Defined as blood pressure above goal despite three antihypertensive
agents of different classes, one of which is a diuretic, at maximally
tolerated doses. Evaluate for secondary causes and medication
non-adherence before adding a fourth agent (commonly spironolactone).
